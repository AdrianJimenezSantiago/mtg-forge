Coated_Fogra39L_VIGC_300.icc
============================

CMYK output profile for ISO 12647-2 paper type 1/2 (coated), built by VIGC on
the FOGRA39 characterization data. Total area coverage 300 %, maximum GCR.

Source: https://registry.color.org/profile-registry/Coated_Fogra39L_VIGC_300

License, as published by the ICC registry: the profile "may be used, embedded,
exchanged, and shared without restriction". It "may not be altered or sold
without written permission of VIGC". This file is distributed unmodified.

SHA-256: 6df43849a84d2632e7900a125f657bd09364ed56775bc4740c1e39a5492d47e3

MPC Forge uses it to separate card images into CMYK for print shops that ask
for Coated FOGRA39 (see mpc_forge/services/printing/print_shops.py).
